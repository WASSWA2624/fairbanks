## 2. What this Handbook is (and is not)

### What it is
This Handbook is the **practical operating companion** to the FairBanks Blueprint.  
It turns Blueprint vision into day-to-day implementation tools: stage gates, checklists, role guidance, meeting rhythms, and crosswalks.

It is **not** a second Blueprint.  
It does not replace the Manifesto, Declaration, or Covenant.  
It does not invent new strategy. Where detail is needed, users return to the Blueprint chapter named in each section.

**Rule:** Strategy lives in the Blueprint. Execution lives in this Handbook.

## 5. Five-stage transformation operating guide (2027–2032)

> The roadmap is time-bound, but transformation is capability-driven.

| Period | Stage | Emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| 2027 | Foundation | Build the institution before scaling it |
| 2028 | Integration | Integrate services, systems, and pathways |
| 2029 | Innovation | Strengthen learning, digital intelligence, and improvement |
| 2030–2031 | Leadership | Deepen influence, partnerships, and institutional leadership |
| 2032 | Sustainability | Consolidate stewardship, resilience, and lasting impact |

**Keep 2030–2031 as a two-year Leadership stage.** Do not compress it into one year merely to force a five-year calendar label.

### Five implementation principles (use at every gate)
1. **Build Strong Foundations Before Expansion**  
2. **Strengthen Systems Before Scaling Services**  
3. **Promote Partnerships Before Duplication**  
4. **Measure Progress Before Accelerating Growth**  
5. **Pursue Sustainability Before Replication**  

### Transformation Gate (mandatory before stage progression)
FairBanks should not advance merely because a calendar year has ended. Before moving stage, management must present Board-ready evidence that:

1. Priority deliverables for the current stage are substantially complete  
2. Risks are identified and mitigated  
3. Financing and workforce can sustain the next stage  
4. MEL indicators show acceptable progress or an explicit recovery plan  
5. Patient safety / clinical quality standards are not compromised by expansion  

**Gate decision options:** Proceed · Proceed with conditions · Hold and remediate · Redesign scope

## 6. Stage playbooks (quick operating cards)

### Stage 1 — 2027 Foundation
**Goal:** Establish governance, systems, people, finance, digital, and operational foundations.  
**Focus actions:**
- Implement FITF layers and core policies/SOPs  
- Strengthen financial controls and reporting cadence  
- Operationalise FCHIP foundations (phased; do not over-claim live capability)  
- Expand primary/preventive services carefully  
- Set institutional baselines and partnership framework  
**Exit test:** Foundations are real enough to support Integration without unsafe scale-up.

### Stage 2 — 2028 Integration
**Goal:** Integrate clinical, community, digital, and workforce pathways into one coordinated model.  
**Focus actions:**
- Strengthen multidisciplinary care and FIHE patient journey continuity  
- Integrate community health with facility pathways  
- Improve patient experience and quality improvement loops  
- Deepen data use for decisions  
**Exit test:** Patients and teams experience one system, not siloed units.

### Stage 3 — 2029 Innovation
**Goal:** Use learning, research, and responsible digital intelligence to improve care and operations.  
**Focus actions:**
- Advance FCHIP analytics use-cases under clinical governance  
- Strengthen research/education partnerships  
- Improve maturity scores on priority FMM dimensions  
**Exit test:** Innovation improves outcomes or efficiency with documented learning.

### Stage 4 — 2030–2031 Leadership
**Goal:** Strengthen FairBanks as a trusted institutional leader and partner.  
**Focus actions:**
- Scale proven models with partners (not duplication)  
- Strengthen influence, brand integrity, and policy dialogue  
- Build leadership bench strength and succession practice  
**Exit test:** FairBanks can lead collaboratively without depending on founder-only capacity.

### Stage 5 — 2032 Sustainability
**Goal:** Lock in stewardship, resilience, and lasting community value.  
**Focus actions:**
- Apply FSSF / investment prioritisation / sustainability compass  
- Confirm financial and operational resilience  
- Refresh Covenant commitments for the next horizon  
**Exit test:** The institution can endure beyond one planning cycle.

## 8. Operating rhythm (recommended)

### Weekly (management)
- Patient safety / quality exceptions  
- Service continuity and staffing risks  
- Priority project blockers  

### Monthly (executive)
- Stage deliverable progress  
- Finance & cash / sustainability signals  
- Partnership and CHIP/FCHIP milestones  
- Risk heat-map update  

### Quarterly (Board pack)
- Transformation Gate evidence (if stage boundary approaches)  
- MEL dashboard + FMM snapshot  
- Material risks and mitigations  
- Investment decisions requiring stewardship review  

### Annually
- Stage review against Blueprint Ch 10  
- Policy/SOP refresh  
- Covenant reaffirmation  
- Horizon planning for the next year within 2027–2032

## 12. Closing rule for Handbook users

When unsure, ask three questions:

1. **Does the Blueprint already decide this?** If yes, implement — do not reopen.
