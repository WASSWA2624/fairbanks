# Executive Summary

FairBanks Medical Centre respectfully proposes a strategic partnership with Victoria University to establish a collaborative model that integrates healthcare service delivery, academic excellence, research, innovation, and community engagement.

At the heart of this proposal is the **FairBanks Community Health Improvement Programme (FCHIP)**—our flagship community engagement platform designed to improve primary healthcare through preventive health services, community outreach, research, skills development, and strategic partnerships.

By combining Victoria University's strengths in education, innovation, and research with FairBanks Medical Centre's practical healthcare platform and community presence, this partnership will create meaningful opportunities for students, faculty, researchers, healthcare professionals, and the communities we serve.

Together, we can develop a replicable University–Community Health Partnership Model that advances healthcare, education, innovation, and sustainable development in Uganda.

---

# 1. About FairBanks Medical Centre

FairBanks Medical Centre is a community-based private primary healthcare facility located in Kyebando–Kisalosalo, Kampala. Guided by our motto, **"Your Health, Our Mission,"** we provide accessible, affordable, and quality healthcare while promoting preventive health and community well-being.

Beyond clinical care, the Centre is committed to addressing broader social determinants of health through partnerships, innovation, research, and community engagement.

---

# 2. The FairBanks Community Health Improvement Programme (FCHIP)

The **FairBanks Community Health Improvement Programme (FCHIP)** is the Centre's flagship community engagement initiative implemented through the FairBanks Social Enterprise.

FCHIP was established to strengthen family and community primary healthcare by bringing together healthcare providers, universities, government institutions, development partners, corporate organisations, and local communities.

The programme focuses on:

* Preventive healthcare.
* Community outreach.
* Health education.
* Early disease detection.
* Maternal and child health.
* Non-communicable disease prevention.
* Elderly care and healthy ageing.
* Community-based research.
* Digital health innovation.
* Skills development and student experiential learning.

FCHIP provides a practical platform where education, research, innovation, and healthcare service delivery work together to improve lives.

---

# 3. Why Victoria University?

Victoria University has distinguished itself as an institution committed to practical learning, innovation, entrepreneurship, and community transformation.

These values closely align with FairBanks Medical Centre's vision.

We believe the University can play a transformative role as FCHIP's founding academic partner by contributing academic expertise, research capacity, student engagement, and innovation while benefiting from a real-world environment for experiential learning and applied research.

# 8. Long-Term Vision

Our vision is to establish **FCHIP as Uganda's leading University–Community Health Partnership Model**, demonstrating how academia, healthcare providers, government, development partners, and communities can work together to improve lives.
