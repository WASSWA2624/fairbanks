## 5. Answers to likely questions

Use plain language. Keep answers short, then offer one example.

### Q1. Tell us about yourself.

**Answer:**
I am a biomedical engineer with over 7 years of hospital and field work. I install, commission, maintain, and train users on medical equipment. I worked at Norvik Hospital, then spent four years as Biomedical Manager at International Hospital Kampala, then managed programmes for the Gould Family Foundation. Since March 2025 I have been on personal contracts — including lab, theatre, and radiology installs in Congo, among them the hospital that serves the Kibali Gold Mine. I am applying because Medequip’s work matches what I already do on site.

### Q2. Why do you want this job / why Medequip?

**Answer:**
Medequip supplies equipment and supports hospitals with install, service, and training across Rwanda and the region. That is exactly my work: show up, install or repair, train the team, and leave clear service notes. I want to join a regional supplier team in Kigali, travel for clients, and grow through your international product training.

### Q3. Do you meet the diploma requirement?

**Answer:**
Yes. I hold a **BSc in Biomedical Engineering from Makerere University (2012–2016)**. That is above the diploma minimum in the advert.

### Q4. How much experience do you have? The advert says 1–2 years.

**Answer:**
I have **over 7 years** of relevant install and maintenance experience — more than the minimum. I am happy to do hands-on field work: installs, preventive and corrective maintenance, training, and reports.

### Q5. What equipment can you handle?

**Answer:**

- **Lab:** haematology analysers, chemistry analysers, blood bank machines
- **Imaging:** CT, X-ray, ultrasound, C-arm
- **Theatre / ICU:** theatre systems, ventilators, monitors, infusion pumps
- **Support plant:** oxygen plant, RO water
- **Maternity / neonatal:** incubators, phototherapy, foetal Doppler, CTG

At IHK I led installs across lab, theatre, ICU, and radiology.

### Q6. Can you travel within Rwanda and across borders?

**Answer:**
Yes. I already travel for field jobs. I have worked in **Uganda, Congo, Kenya, Tanzania, and Somaliland**. I am ready to relocate to Kigali and travel for Medequip assignments.

### Q7. How do you train hospital staff?

**Answer:**
After install or repair, I do a practical handover: show correct use and basic care, answer questions on site, and leave the team able to run the device safely. I have trained engineers, technicians, and clinical users.

### Q8. How do you document your work?

**Answer:**
I write clear service and maintenance notes after each job — what was done, what was found, and what the client should watch. At IHK I also supported service records and COHSASA accreditation paperwork.

### Q9. Give an example of a hard field job.

**Answer:**
On personal contracts in Congo I completed lab, theatre, and radiology installs at several hospitals, including the hospital that serves the Kibali Gold Mine. That meant travel to site, install and commission, train users, and hand over with documentation.

### Q10. Are you willing to attend international training?

**Answer:**
Yes. I want to learn Medequip’s product lines properly. Continuous training is part of how I stay useful to clients.

### Q11. How is your English?

**Answer:**
I speak clear, fluent English for client work, training, and reports. Luganda is my native language.

### Q12. When can you start / will you move to Kigali?

**Answer:**
I am ready to relocate to Kigali for this role. Start date can follow your interview and offer process; I can discuss exact timing once we talk.

### Q13. What is your current work situation?

**Answer:**
Since March 2025 I work on personal biomedical contracts for hospitals that call me in. Before that I was Biomedical Programs Manager at Gould Family Foundation (Aug 2024–Feb 2025), and before that Biomedical Manager at IHK (Jan 2020–Jan 2024).

### Q14. What salary do you expect?

**Answer:**
I am open and flexible. I care most about the role, travel support, and training. I am happy to hear Medequip’s band for this post and then discuss a fair package.

*(Only name a number if they press you — research Rwanda market rates for biomedical field engineers before the interview.)*

### Q15. Do you have any questions for us?

**Ask 2–3 of these:**

1. Which brands and product lines would I support first?
2. How is travel planned for upcountry and cross-border jobs?
3. What does the first 90 days of training look like?
4. Who would I report to day to day?
5. How do you measure a successful service visit?
